  from: https://6502disassembly.com/va-asteroids/:
  "When jumping into hyperspace, a random number from 0-31 is generated. 
  If it's 24-31 (25%), the jump fails and the ship is destroyed. 
  The random value is reduced to 0-7, doubled, and increased by 4 (4 - 18). 
  If that value is less than the current number of asteroids on screen, 
  the jump fails, which means the chance of failure increases as the screen gets more crowded. 
  If the jump succeeds, the game will wait until there are no asteroids 
  in the immediate vicinity of the spawn-in point. 
  The position is random, but limited so that the ship doesn't appear near the edges of the screen."
